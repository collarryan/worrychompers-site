Or use Send feedback in the app, under Help & privacy in the grown-ups area. It adds your app and phone version for you.

What to write to us about

  • Something not working

    Tell us what you were doing when it happened and which phone you have. If you send a screenshot, check it doesn’t show any of your child’s worries.

  • Ideas

    New parts for the Chompers, new ways to get a worry out, or anything you wish the app did.

  • Feedback

    What’s working for your family, and what isn’t.
